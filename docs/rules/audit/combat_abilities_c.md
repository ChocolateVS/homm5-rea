# Combat audit — activated creature abilities (cast switch)

Per-ability rows merged into `docs/rules/AUDIT_COMBAT.md` by `tools/re/audit/ledger.py` (rows here override the
computed status). Exe references are H5_Game.exe addresses (Ghidra 12.1.4).

Activated abilities are spells (`SPELL_ABILITY_*`) executed by the cast switch `FUN_00984a00`: jump table at
`009886d4`, byte index table at `00988790` (index = spell id − 0x9b). Case entry points used below:
0x9d Resurrect Allies `00986fe0`, 0xa1 Summon Balor `0098722d` (→ `FUN_00861d30`), 0xa2 Explosion `009875db`,
0xaa Harm Touch `0098747f`, 0xab Mana Feed `00987b0e`, 0x9e Scatter Shot `00987788`, 0x13f Syphon Mana `00985e0d`,
0x140 Leap `009881e0` (→ `FUN_00979930`), 0x141 Summon Other `00985cf1`, 0x143 Treant Union `00986377`,
0x144 Ammo Steal `009865a0`, 0x148 Seduce `00985b4e`, 0x149 Rage of the Forest `00985a42`, 0x14d Power Feed
`00985559`, 0x15a/0x15b Pack Dive `009882e0`/`009883f2`, 0xb0 Dash `00987e8a`, 0xde Call Lightning `00987ecb`, 0x14e Call Storm
`00988279`, 0x13e Scavenger `0098624f`, 0x12d Set Snares `0098614d`, 0x14c Luck Gambler `00985705`, 0x145 Order of the
Chief `009867fb`, 0x12b/0x12c Sacrifice/Swallow Goblin `009869e0`, 0xf7 Stormbolt `00988083`, 0x14a Sabotage `00985983`.
Note: float→int conversions here are FISTP under `OR AH,0x0C` (truncation) unless stated. Ability charges are the creature's `SpellPoints1`
(object from vf 0x6c, vf 0x2c get / vf 0x30 set); the cast decrements them. Area shapes come from the spell area
switch `FUN_0097a530`.
