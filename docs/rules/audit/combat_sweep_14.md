Per-ID rows merged into `docs/rules/AUDIT_COMBAT.md` by `tools/re/audit/ledger.py` (fragments are read in natural
order, so rows here override sweep 1–13). Exe references are H5_Game.exe (GOG 3.1) addresses, analysed headless with
