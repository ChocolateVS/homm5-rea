# Combat audit — sweep 4: magic, rune and might-over-magic hero perks

Per-ID rows merged into `docs/rules/AUDIT_COMBAT.md` by `tools/re/audit/ledger.py` (rows here override the earlier
fragments). Exe references are H5_Game.exe (GOG 3.1) addresses, analysed headless with Ghidra 12.1.4 (private project
copy). Code: `src/H5.Combat/Battle.Sweep4.cs` (+ small hunks); tests: `tests/H5.Combat.Tests/AuditSweep4Tests.cs`.

How the perks are reached: most of these perks have no `HasSkill` call with an immediate id in combat code. They act
through (a) the hero's spell book, built on the adventure side by FUN_00b54770 (perk acquired: switch on the perk —
Master of Curses/Mind/Pain/Blessing/Wrath/Abjuration (0x2e..0x33) add the mass version FUN_00a33eb0 of every known spell
whose mass perk FUN_00a33cf0 is that perk; Empowered Spells (0x46) adds FUN_00a34060 of every known spell; Power of Haste
(0x8f) / Stone (0x90) add Mass Haste / Mass Stoneskin unconditionally (MOV ECX,0x18 / 0x19 @ 00b55064 / 00b55095);
Magic Bond (0x43) → SPELL_ABILITY_MAGIC_BOND 0x3f, Spirit Link (0x6c) → SPELL_SPIRIT_LINK 0x3a, Unsummon (0x88) →
SPELL_ABILITY_UNSUMMON 0x42, Teleport Assault (0x8a) → SPELL_TELEPORT) and FUN_00b4bb40 (spell learnt: empowered / mass
version when the perk is owned); (b) the hero's skill-level slot vf 0x174 on the CHero object (e.g. 00b4b4c8) and the
combat hero's HasSkill vf 0x290 = FUN_00bbfed0 (skill level > 0); a combat creature's vf 0x290 is FUN_00a332c0 = false.
